# About this website

When I first read this book a few years ago, I was deeply touched by the depth and humanity behind its pages.

Being an IT professional in real life sometimes helps, and this is why I decided to create this small space to share opinions and knowledge,  
and to keep a <i>memoire</i> of a man who lived in the past century.

This should be intended as a simple tribute, homage or experiment by an amateur to Edgar Lee Masters' work.

# Features

The website is built with Hugo and the Ed theme.  
It simply displays a random poem every time you refresh or visit the homepage and it is meant as a tiny dedicated space to read a poem daily.
